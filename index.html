FAQ

Before you request a clean.

Is HomeEase JA already operating everywhere?

No. This page is designed for an early pilot. We will confirm whether your area is currently supported before scheduling any service.

Are the prices final?

No. The prices shown are pilot starting points. Final quotes may vary based on size, condition, travel, special requests and add-ons.

Do I need to be in Jamaica to request a clean?

No. One of the use cases we are exploring is allowing people abroad to arrange cleaning for family members and properties in Jamaica.

What makes the service different?

We are building around standardized cleaning checklists, clearer service scope, cleaner vetting, reliable scheduling and a mobile-first customer experience rather than an informal referral-only process.

Will I have to download an app?

No. The initial experience is designed to work well from a mobile browser. A native app would only make sense after the service has proven strong repeat usage.

How will payment work?

Payment methods are still being validated. No payment is collected through this prototype page. Any paid pilot service should have its final price and payment method confirmed directly with the customer first.
